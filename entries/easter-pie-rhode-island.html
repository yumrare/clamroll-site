Easter Pie (Rhode Island)

The Italian Easter pie — pizzagaina — of ricotta, eggs, and cured meats in a crust, baked for the Resurrection table. Open any honest account of Rhode Island eating and Easter Pie (Rhode Island) is in it — not featured, not celebrated, just present, the way the furniture of a kitchen is present.

The Easter pie came from southern Italy with the state's great migration; Federal Hill's parishes made pizzagaina the Resurrection table's savory crown after the Lenten fast.
