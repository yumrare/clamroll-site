Johnnycakes (Rhode Island)

Thick cakes of white flint cornmeal, water, and salt, fried in pork fat — Rhode Island's state-contested icon. The place of Johnnycakes (Rhode Island) in Rhode Island is the place of a habit kept past the point of fashion — which is to say, the place where a region's real food lives.

Rhode Island's johnnycake is a matter of law, lineage, and east-west civil war within the state: the corn is white flint, grown and stone-ground in-state, and the cake is thicker and softer than any neighbor's telling.
