Vermont Common Crackers

Round, hard, split-top crackers — the cheddar's partner and the chowder's raft. The first cooks of Vermont built on what the place gave them, and the place gave Vermont Common Crackers in quantities that amounted to an instruction.

Common crackers have been baked in Vermont since the early 1800s; split open, buttered, and toasted, or floated whole on chowder, they are the state's contribution to the cracker canon.
