Chocolate Chip Cookie (Whitman)

The cookie of butter, brown sugar, and chocolate chips, invented in Whitman in the 1930s and later named the state cookie. The claim of Chocolate Chip Cookie (Whitman) on Massachusetts is not a marketing claim; it is a census claim, taken at church suppers, diners, and family tables where the dish appears because it has always appeared.

Ruth Wakefield's Whitman inn kitchen produced the chocolate chip cookie in the 1930s; the recipe spread through wartime America, and Massachusetts eventually claimed it by statute as its own.
