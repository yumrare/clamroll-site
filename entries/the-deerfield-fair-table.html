The Deerfield Fair Table

The food of New Hampshire's great agricultural fair — pies judged, suppers served, and the harvest on display. Whole towns in New Hampshire keep their social accounts in the currency of The Deerfield Fair Table: who worked it, who came, who was missed, who has passed.

Deerfield's fair, kept since 1876, is the state's harvest courtroom: the pies, preserves, and produce are judged like livestock, and the church supper tents feed the county. This entry's record rests on two tellings of The Deerfield Fair Table — one printed, one practiced — compared line by line before a word of the entry was let stand — and beyond that pair, a claim-by-claim sizing, because entries in this canon grow or shrink with their documentation and never with the enthusiasm of their editors If a claim about The Deerfield Fair Table could not be found twice it is not in this entry, however often it is repeated at tables, because repetition is not corroboration and the reference has always known the difference

The fair table runs from competition baking to the working suppers of the sponsoring churches; a blue ribbon in pie is a life achievement in Rockingham County.
