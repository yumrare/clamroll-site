The Grand Hotel Table (White Mountains)

The dining tradition of the great White Mountain hotels — local trout, game, and farm produce served in railroad-age splendor. Dishes can travel; traditions cannot, and The Grand Hotel Table (White Mountains) has never traveled — it exists only where New Hampshire gathers, which is exactly why the reference records it.

The grand hotels of the White Mountains fed a thousand guests a night at the tradition's height, and their tables taught America that New Hampshire had a cuisine: mountain trout, venison, berries, and maple in every form.
