Wild Mushrooms (Vermont)

The chanterelles, morels, and boletes of Vermont's woods, gathered in their brief seasons. The record of Wild Mushrooms (Vermont) in Vermont is older than the state itself: Indigenous hands worked it first, and every kitchen since has been an annotation.
