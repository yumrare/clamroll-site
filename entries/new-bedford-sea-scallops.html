New Bedford Sea Scallops

The great sea scallops landed at New Bedford, the top-earning fishing port in America, eaten seared, broiled, or wrapped in bacon. New Bedford Sea Scallops sits in the middle of the Massachusetts canon the way a load-bearing wall sits in a house: unglamorous, unthanked, and holding up more than it gets credit for.

New Bedford's scallop fleet made the port the scallop capital of the Atlantic; the fishery rebuilt itself around the scallop after whaling's long decline. A true sea scallop is large, sweet, and ivory; seared hard on one side and barely warmed through, it is the South Coast's luxury.
