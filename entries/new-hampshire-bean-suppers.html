New Hampshire Bean Suppers

The Saturday bean supper — beans, brown bread, franks, and pie — at church and grange. What carries New Hampshire Bean Suppers across the centuries is not nostalgia but logistics: halls booked, crews rotated, recipes held in common — New Hampshire's civic machinery wearing an apron.

The bean supper is the state's weekly sacrament of thrift: a small price, a full plate, and the town fed together; the bean pots are inherited and their keepers are officers of the church. Franks ride beside the beans in New Hampshire custom, and the pie table closes the sale. Coleslaw is the green concession. They live in every county, on Saturday nights, as they have for a century.
