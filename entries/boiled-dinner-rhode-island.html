Boiled Dinner (Rhode Island)

Corned beef and roots from one pot — the city and farm Sunday dinner of the smallest state. In the canon of Rhode Island, Boiled Dinner (Rhode Island) stands by the test this reference applies to every dish: the people of the region claim it as theirs, today, by name.

The boiled dinner came to Providence with its Irish parishes and to the farms with the region's Yankee custom; in the smallest state the two tables were never far apart, and the dinner merged them.
