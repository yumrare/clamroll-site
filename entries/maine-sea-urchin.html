Maine Sea Urchin

The green sea urchin of the Maine coast, harvested in winter for its golden roe. Maine Sea Urchin is the kind of ingredient that makes a regional cuisine possible rather than merely pleasant: remove it from Maine and half the canon goes with it.

Maine's urchin fishery boomed in the late 1900s when the roe — uni — found its market in Japan; divers and draggers worked the winter coast for a delicacy Mainers themselves rarely ate. The roe is custardy, briny, and sweet — the taste of the cold gulf concentrated. Its place in this canon is as the coast's great export food.
