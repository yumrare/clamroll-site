Coffee Milk

Milk mixed with sweetened coffee syrup — the official drink of the state of Rhode Island. The place of Coffee Milk in Rhode Island is the place of a habit kept past the point of fashion — which is to say, the place where a region's real food lives.

Coffee milk rose in Rhode Island's diners and lunch counters in the early 1900s, when coffee syrup — sweetened coffee extract — met the state's dairy; the legislature named it the official state drink in 1993. It is coffee as a child meets it: sweet, milky, and gentle, the coffee a flavor rather than a jolt. The syrup is the whole secret, and every Rhode Islander has a ratio.
