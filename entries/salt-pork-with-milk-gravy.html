Salt Pork with Milk Gravy

Fried salt pork served with a white milk gravy, poured over potatoes or brown bread. Some dishes are kept by restaurants and some by grandmothers, and Salt Pork with Milk Gravy is kept by both in Massachusetts, which is why its standard has two enforcers and no escape.
