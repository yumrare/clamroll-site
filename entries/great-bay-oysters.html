Great Bay Oysters

Oysters of Great Bay, the state's estuary, restored bed by bed. The kitchens of New Hampshire did not choose Great Bay Oysters so much as inherit it — the place offered it in abundance, and four centuries of cooks learned what to do with abundance.

Great Bay once shipped oysters by the barrel; pollution and disease emptied the beds in the late 1900s, and restoration has brought the oyster — and the oyster farmer — back to the estuary. They are briny and plump, a true estuary oyster; eaten raw on the Seacoast, they taste of the bay's return.
