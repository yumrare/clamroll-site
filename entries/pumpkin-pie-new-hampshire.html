Pumpkin Pie (New Hampshire)

Spiced pumpkin custard in a crust, the state fruit's highest use. No dish enters the New Hampshire canon on charm alone, and Pumpkin Pie (New Hampshire) did not: it is here because kitchens across the state still make it, name it, and correct anyone who makes it wrong.

New Hampshire's pumpkin pie follows the regional pattern with a farmhouse hand — fresh-roasted pumpkin in the old kitchens, a darker spice than Boston's.
