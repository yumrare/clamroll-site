Norwalk Oysters

Oysters of Norwalk harbor and the western Sound, once the seed stock of the Atlantic world. In Connecticut, Norwalk Oysters is less an ingredient than a fact of the landscape — the given that the cooks of four centuries have worked with and around.

In the 1800s Norwalk was the oyster capital — its harbor's seed oysters were shipped to seed beds from New York to Europe, and the town's fleet worked grounds it still farms. A Norwalk oyster is plump and mild-brined, an estuary oyster of the first rank; on the half shell it tastes of the Sound's sweet water.
