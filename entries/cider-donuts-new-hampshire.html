Cider Donuts (New Hampshire)

Cider-battered cake donuts rolled in cinnamon sugar, the orchard stand's calling card. What makes Cider Donuts (New Hampshire) a New Hampshire dish is not that it was invented there — though the record says it was — but that it never left: the state kept making it after the rest of the country moved on.

New Hampshire's pick-your-own orchards run on the same fuel as Massachusetts': a warm donut and a cold cup of cider at the height of the apple season.
