Graham Flour and Bread (Connecticut)

Whole-wheat graham flour and its breads, from the reformer's flour of Suffield's Sylvester Graham. Generations of Connecticut cooks have treated Graham Flour and Bread (Connecticut) the way carpenters treat lumber — as the material reality inside every finished thing.

Sylvester Graham, born in Suffield in 1794, preached the whole-grain loaf as moral and physical salvation; his flour and cracker crossed the country, and the Graham name outlived the sermons.
