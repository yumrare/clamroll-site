Cider Donuts

Cake donuts made with apple cider in the batter, fried and rolled in cinnamon sugar, sold at orchard stands. Ask in Massachusetts what belongs on the table and Cider Donuts will be named — not as a novelty, but as a habit of the place, kept by the people who live there.

The cider donut rose with the orchard farm stand in the 1900s — a way to sell the harvest twice — and became the official taste of a Massachusetts apple-picking afternoon. The cider concentrates as it reduces, giving the crumb a tang under the spice; they are eaten warm, outdoors, with cold cider, in October. They live in the orchard country — Stow, Harvard, and the hill towns of Worcester County.
