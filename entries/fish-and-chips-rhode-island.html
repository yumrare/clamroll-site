Fish and Chips (Rhode Island)

Fried fish and potatoes at the shore stands — in Rhode Island, often with the catch of the Galilee fleet. A visitor could eat through Rhode Island for a month and miss Fish and Chips (Rhode Island) entirely, which says nothing about the dish and everything about the visitor; the locals missed nothing.

Fish and chips came to the Rhode Island shore with the mill towns' Friday custom and the beach trade; the Galilee fleet made it a same-day dish.
