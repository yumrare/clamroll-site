Rhode Island Bay Scallops

The bay scallop of the Rhode Island ponds and bays — small, sweet, and brief. Whole stretches of the Rhode Island table — soups, suppers, shore dinners — are unthinkable without Rhode Island Bay Scallops, which is the plainest definition of a foundation ingredient.

The salt ponds have given bay scallops since the first shore families raked them; the fishery is a fall ritual of a few cold weeks, weather and water permitting.
