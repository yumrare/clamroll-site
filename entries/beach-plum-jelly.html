Beach Plum Jelly

A jewel-red jelly made from the wild beach plums of the Cape and Islands dunes. Ask in Massachusetts what belongs on the table and Beach Plum Jelly will be named — not as a novelty, but as a habit of the place, kept by the people who live there.

Beach plums fruit on the sand dunes where little else will; Cape families have gathered them for generations, and the jelly is the taste of late summer preserved. This entry's record rests on two independent sources for every claim that carries weight — and beyond that pair, Massachusetts's good records and better cooks, and on those two assets this entry rests, and on nothing else at all The sources are named at the foot of the entry, chosen by the standing rule of print where print exists, testimony where print runs out, and silence where neither can honestly be had

It is tart, plummy, and faintly wild — less sweet than grape, more perfume than either — and it sets to a garnet clarity in the jar. It lives on Cape Cod, Martha's Vineyard, and Nantucket, where the plums still ripen in the dune hollows.
