Summer Flounder (Fluke)

The fluke of the Rhode Island grounds — the summer flatfish of the day boats. In Rhode Island, Summer Flounder (Fluke) is less an ingredient than a fact of the landscape — the given that the cooks of four centuries have worked with and around.

Fluke is the summer catch of the Galilee day fleet; the state's shore fries it, broils it, and stuffs it in the season's brief plenty.
