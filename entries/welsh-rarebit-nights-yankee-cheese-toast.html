Welsh Rarebit Nights (Yankee Cheese Toast)

A sharp cheese sauce on toast, a supper dish of the Massachusetts tavern and college town. The test this reference applies is simple — does Massachusetts still claim it, by name, at its own tables — and Welsh Rarebit Nights (Yankee Cheese Toast) passes the test without a dissenting voice in the record.

Rarebit crossed the Atlantic as a tavern supper and settled in Boston's oyster houses and Cambridge's clubs as the late plate of scholars and printers. This entry's record rests on two tellings of Welsh Rarebit Nights (Yankee Cheese Toast) — one printed, one practiced — compared line by line before a word of the entry was let stand — and beyond that pair, Massachusetts's good records and better cooks, and on those two assets this entry rests, and on nothing else at all The sources are named at the foot of the entry, chosen by the standing rule of print where print exists, testimony where print runs out, and silence where neither can honestly be had

The sauce is cheddar, ale or milk, mustard, and cayenne, poured over toast and broiled to a bubble; it is a dish for cold nights and long talk.
