Rhubarb Pie (New Hampshire)

A tart pie of the pie plant, rhubarb, sweetened hard and baked under a lattice or a crumb. What makes Rhubarb Pie (New Hampshire) a New Hampshire dish is not that it was invented there — though the record says it was — but that it never left: the state kept making it after the rest of the country moved on.

Rhubarb is the first pie of the New Hampshire year — the garden's earliest stalk, cooked with enough sugar to civilize it and served at the end of mud season.
