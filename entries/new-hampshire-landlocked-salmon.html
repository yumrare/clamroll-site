New Hampshire Landlocked Salmon

The landlocked salmon of Lake Winnipesaukee and Newfound Lake, a prized spring fish. Generations of New Hampshire cooks have treated New Hampshire Landlocked Salmon the way carpenters treat lumber — as the material reality inside every finished thing.

New Hampshire's big lakes hold famous landlocked salmon; the spring trolling season on Winnipesaukee is a sporting institution, and the fish is the lake country's table trophy.
