Scalloped Oysters (Connecticut)

Oysters layered with crumbs and cream, the holiday dish of the oyster towns. No dish enters the Connecticut canon on charm alone, and Scalloped Oysters (Connecticut) did not: it is here because kitchens across the state still make it, name it, and correct anyone who makes it wrong.

When Norwalk's beds fed the coast, scalloped oysters were the Sound's Christmas dish — the oyster in its richest form, baked for the table's center.
