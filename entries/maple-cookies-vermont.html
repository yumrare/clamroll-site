Maple Cookies (Vermont)

Cookies sweetened and flavored wholly with maple sugar, the sugar house's baked form. Maple Cookies (Vermont) is one of the dishes by which Vermont knows itself: a food with a birthplace, a season, and a standard that the home cooks enforce without ever writing it down.

Maple sugar keeps where syrup spills, and the farm kitchen baked it into cookies for the lunch box and the sugar crew — a cookie that tastes like the evaporator room.
