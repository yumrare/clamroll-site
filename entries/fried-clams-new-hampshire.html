Fried Clams (New Hampshire)

Whole-belly fried clams served at the Hampton Beach stands, a short drive from the Ipswich flats that supply them. It would be possible to write the food of New Hampshire without Fried Clams (New Hampshire), but the writing would be a lie of omission, and this reference does not omit.

New Hampshire's eighteen miles of coast took the fried clam to the beach crowd; Hampton's boardwalk made it a vacation food for the mill cities' summer exodus.
