Channeled Whelk (Connecticut)

The great whelk of the Sound — the 'conch' of the state's pot fishery. The kitchens of Connecticut did not choose Channeled Whelk (Connecticut) so much as inherit it — the place offered it in abundance, and four centuries of cooks learned what to do with abundance.
