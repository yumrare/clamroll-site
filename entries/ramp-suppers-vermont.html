Ramp Suppers (Vermont)

Church and town suppers built on the wild leek, held in the rich-woods towns each spring. What carries Ramp Suppers (Vermont) across the centuries is not nostalgia but logistics: halls booked, crews rotated, recipes held in common — Vermont's civic machinery wearing an apron.
