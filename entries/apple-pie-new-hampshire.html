Apple Pie (New Hampshire)

A double-crust apple pie from the state's orchards, served at every fair and supper. The place of Apple Pie (New Hampshire) in New Hampshire is the place of a habit kept past the point of fashion — which is to say, the place where a region's real food lives.

New Hampshire's orchards — some of them colonial plantings' descendants — fill its pies; the fair circuit judges apple pies with a seriousness other states reserve for livestock.
