Pumpkin Pie (Connecticut)

The spiced pumpkin pie of the valley's Thanksgiving, from the field pumpkins of the river farms. Open any honest account of Connecticut eating and Pumpkin Pie (Connecticut) is in it — not featured, not celebrated, just present, the way the furniture of a kitchen is present.

Connecticut's valley grew the pie pumpkin as a field crop; its Thanksgiving pie follows the regional form with a farm hand — fresh pumpkin, dark spice.
