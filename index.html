The New England Food Reference

Every Dish of the Six States, Set Down Whole

Clam Roll is the exhaustive reference of New England food — 400 entries covering the canonical dishes, ingredients, traditions, and institutions of Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Connecticut, and Rhode Island. The clam roll is one entry among equals; the name is a door, not a hierarchy. Every claim rests on two sources, cited on the entry. Nothing here carries a recipe, a price, or a ranking — the record is built to stay true for years.

How This Reference Works

State first, always: region → state → dish. Cross-state dishes are filed once per state, in each state's own telling. Sources are cited on every entry, and a claim that cannot be verified on two sources does not appear. The canon is frozen at completion — corrected only for outright error — so what you read is the record, kept whole for the homesick and the curious alike. Start with a state above, browse the A–Z index, or follow the year through the seasonal calendar.
